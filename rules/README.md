# Rules

Status: **CONCEPT catalog; no executable CARE rules**.

Follow the [rule contract](../docs/RULE_CONTRACT.md): stable identities, revisions,
applicability, required facts, provenance, health effects, repair metadata, and
verification. Evaluation interprets explicit evidence without live probes or
mutation. A finding does not grant repair authority.

Only reviewed incident knowledge with verified causal/repair evidence and
synthetic regression cases may become an operational rule. Preserve negative,
healthy, unknown, conflicting, stale, and out-of-scope cases as relevant.
Do not import CWRE rule numbers or fabricate a populated rule catalog at Genesis.
